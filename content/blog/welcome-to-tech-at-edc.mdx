Tech@EDC is a new home for anyone at Edmonds College who wants to build things with technology. Whether you are deep into a computer science degree or just curious about how the apps on your phone work, there is a place for you here.

## What we do

We learn by making. This year we are running three programs:

- **Build Cohort**: small teams spend a quarter shipping one real project, each with a mentor.
- **Mentorship**: one-on-one conversations with people working in tech.
- **Buildathon**: a 48-hour weekend hackathon open to every skill level.

Between those, expect workshops, project nights, and socials.

## How to get involved

The fastest way in is our Discord. Introduce yourself, tell us what you are interested in, and come to the next event on our Luma calendar. If you want updates in your inbox, subscribe to the newsletter at the bottom of this page.

See you there.
